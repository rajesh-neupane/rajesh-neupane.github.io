**TL;DR:** At Genus ABS, processed ~80M Lely robotic-milking records across 150+ commercial herds to build novel phenotypes and genetic parameters.

## Problem
Sensor streams are massive, messy, and disconnected from breeding decisions.

## What I did
*   Built scalable ETL for high-velocity milking + environment + health data
*   Engineered continuous phenotypes from noisy telemetry
*   Estimated genetic parameters to feed trait selection

## Result
Production-ready phenotypes linking sensor data → genetics → healthier, more efficient herds.

**Stack:** Python, SQL, HPC/Slurm · **Role:** Genetics Data Analytics Intern, Genus ABS
